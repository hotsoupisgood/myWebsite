San Francisco County had the highest share of workers commuting by public transportation in California, 18.7 of 100 workers

29 Oct 2026

Public transportation here means bus, subway, light rail, commuter rail, streetcar and ferry. Taxicabs are not counted. Across California, 2.9 of every 100 workers commuted by public transportation. In 39 of the 58 counties, fewer than 1 of every 100 workers did.

Bars show the survey estimate only. Margins of error are in the data file (CSV). The estimates pool survey responses collected from 2020 to 2024.
